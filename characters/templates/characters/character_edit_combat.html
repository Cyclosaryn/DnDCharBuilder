Quick Tips:
  • Short Rest: Spend hit dice to regain HP. Roll your hit die + CON modifier per die spent.
  • Long Rest: Regain all HP and half of used hit dice (minimum 1). Reset death saves and reduce exhaustion by 1.
  • Temp HP: Doesn't stack - always take the higher value when receiving new temp HP.
  • Death Saves: Reset to 0 when character regains any HP or is stabilized.
